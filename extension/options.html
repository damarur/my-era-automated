The break is unpaid and deducted from the daily worked time. If you already clocked out/in during the day, that gap is used instead. The minimum required break is 30 min; switch the break off for days without one. The daily target is only used to compare against the worked time in the summary.

Auto clock

⚠️ Use at your own risk. Auto clock clocks in and out on your behalf and may not work (Chrome closed or asleep, network or MyEra errors, API changes, wrong schedule). You are solely responsible for your recorded times, so always check MyEra.

Uses the clock in / out times of the weekly schedule (Mon–Fri) and replaces the reminders. You get a browser notification every time it clocks in or out, and also if it is skipped or fails, so you can step in. Right before acting it checks MyEra: it only clocks in if there is no clocking yet today, and only clocks out if you are clocked in. It skips absences and holidays, and never acts more than 10 min after the scheduled time. Chrome must be running.
