The break is unpaid and deducted from the daily worked time. If you already clocked out/in during the day, that gap is used instead. The minimum required break is 30 min; untick Break for days without one. The daily target (default 07:36) is only used to compare against the worked time in the weekly summary.

Times use your computer's local timezone. Reminders only fire while Chrome is running.
